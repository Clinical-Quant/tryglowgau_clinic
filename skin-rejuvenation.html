Treatment Area

Skin rejuvenation.

Restore what time and sun have taken. Treatments designed to improve tone, texture, and clarity — the kind of change that earns the comment “your skin looks amazing,” not “what did you have done?”

What we mean by skin rejuvenation.

Skin rejuvenation is a broad category of treatments that work on the surface and just below it — improving the quality, texture, and appearance of the skin itself, rather than changing facial structure.

For most clients, the goal isn’t dramatic transformation. It’s skin that looks well-rested, healthy, and even — fine lines softened, sun damage reduced, pores refined, tone more uniform. The right treatment plan does this gradually, with little to no downtime.

It’s a journey, not an event. Skin rejuvenation responds best to a sustained plan — a course of treatments over weeks or months, paired with at-home care. We’ll help you build a plan that’s realistic for your life and your goals.

Treatment Types

What we offer in our clinic.

Each of these approaches works differently — your consultation will help us tailor a plan that fits your skin and your schedule.

Chemical Peels

Resurface and refresh.

Professionally applied peels to remove damaged outer layers and stimulate regeneration. Range from gentle refresher peels to deeper corrective options.

Microneedling

Stimulate collagen.

Maintenance & ritual.

Customised facial protocols built around your skin’s current state. A cornerstone of any long-term skin-health routine.

What to expect at your consultation.

Skin rejuvenation works best as a plan, not a one-off. We’ll look at your skin under magnification, talk about what bothers you, and design a course of treatments with realistic expectations for what each one will achieve.

Step 01 — Skin assessment

We examine your skin closely — type, condition, concerns, history. We’ll also talk about your at-home routine and what’s working for you.

Step 02 — Goal setting

What would you like to see change? Texture, tone, pigmentation, scarring, fine lines? We’ll talk through what’s realistic and how long results typically take.

Step 03 — Course design

A plan with treatment types, session frequency, anticipated timeline, and pricing. Adjustable as your skin responds.

Common questions.

How many treatments will I need?

Most skin rejuvenation treatments work as a course — typically 3–6 sessions spaced weeks apart, with maintenance treatments thereafter. We’ll outline a realistic timeline at your consultation.

Is there downtime?

Depends on the treatment. Some options (like light peels or refreshers) involve little to no downtime; others (deeper peels, microneedling) may involve a few days of redness or flaking. We’ll always be upfront about what to expect.

Is it suitable for my skin tone?

Many skin treatments work well across all skin tones, but some need careful handling for darker complexions to avoid pigmentation issues. We’ll assess your skin individually and recommend only what’s safe and effective for you.

Will I see results after one session?

Some treatments show a noticeable difference even after a single session (like a hydrating facial or light peel). Others — particularly microneedling for scarring or deeper peels — need multiple sessions for visible improvement.

What about at-home skincare?

We’ll always talk about at-home care — in-clinic treatments alone won’t get you the results you want without a sensible home routine. We can recommend products, but we don’t sell them — you’re free to buy what works for you.
