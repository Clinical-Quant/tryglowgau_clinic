Common questions.

What happens after I request a consultation?

Our team will be in touch within one business day to confirm a time that works for you, share what to expect, and answer any quick questions before your visit. You’ll hear from Sofia, our client coordinator.

How long is the first consultation?

Typically 45–60 minutes. It’s a real conversation — we don’t rush you through a sales pitch. We’ll talk through your goals, look at your skin or facial structure if relevant, and design a tentative plan together.

Is there a cost for the consultation?

We don’t charge a fee for our initial consultations — they’re an investment in understanding what you need. Any treatment pricing is discussed separately, with full transparency.

Which location should I pick?

All three studios offer the full menu. Old Town is our original and tends to book first; River North is closest to the Loop; Lincoln Park serves the North Side. Choose whichever is most convenient.

Can I bring a support person?

Of course. Many clients find it helpful to have a trusted person with them for the first visit, particularly when discussing treatment options. We’ll always ensure you have private time alone if needed.

What if I’m nervous?

That’s completely normal. Your first consultation carries no obligation to proceed with any treatment. We’ll go at your pace and explain every step before doing anything.
