How We Approach Retail

Recommended, not pushed.

We don’t sell retail products to anyone we haven’t assessed. Our team recommends products alongside a treatment plan — never the other way around.

01

Recommendation, not prescription

We’ll suggest products that fit your skin and your goals — and we’ll be just as happy if you don’t buy anything at all.

02

In-clinic only

The Aurum line is available at all three studios. We don’t sell online — you’ll need to come in, which is intentional. It keeps us in conversation with you.

03

No upselling

If a treatment plan doesn’t need a particular product, we won’t suggest it. If a different brand would suit you better, we’ll say so.

Try Before You Buy

Ask our team at your next visit.

The best way to know if a product is right for you is a proper skin assessment. Add a product consultation to any appointment.
