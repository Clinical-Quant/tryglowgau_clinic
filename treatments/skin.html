Treatment Area

Skin Rejuvenation.

Skin rejuvenation treatments work on the surface and just below it — improving texture, tone, pigmentation, scarring, and overall quality. The result is the kind of change that earns the comment “your skin looks amazing,” not “what did you have done?”

What we mean by skin.

This category includes everything from non-invasive facials (no downtime) to deeper resurfacing treatments (a few days of recovery). The right choice depends on your skin type, your concerns, your tolerance for downtime, and your long-term goals. We’ll help you build a plan that’s realistic for your life.

Skin responds to consistency, not intensity. A 6–12 month plan of appropriate treatments combined with a sensible at-home routine will outperform a single aggressive session every time.

Treatment Types

What’s available in our clinic.

We’ll talk through these in detail during your consultation and recommend what serves your goals — and what doesn’t.

HydraFacial

Cleanse, hydrate, glow.

A patented three-step treatment: gentle exfoliation, painless extraction, and deep serum infusion. No downtime. Suitable for most skin types. Often done monthly as maintenance.

Halo & MOXI

Resurface with lasers.

Halo is a hybrid fractional laser (ablative + non-ablative) for tone, texture, and pigmentation. MOXI is a gentler non-ablative laser for early signs of ageing and maintenance. Both need a few days of social downtime.

Morpheus8

Radiofrequency microneedling.

Combines microneedling with radiofrequency energy to stimulate collagen production at deeper levels. Effective for texture, scarring, and skin tightening. Typically done as a series of 3–4 sessions.

What to expect at your consultation.

Your first visit to Aurum is a conversation — not a sales pitch. We’ll ask what you’d like to explore, look at your skin closely, talk about what’s bothering you, and outline options clearly. If we don’t think treatment is right for you right now, we’ll say so.

Step 01 — Skin assessment

We examine your skin closely — type, condition, concerns, history. We also talk about your at-home routine and what’s working for you.

Step 02 — Goal setting

What would you like to see change? Texture, tone, pigmentation, scarring, fine lines? We’ll talk through what’s realistic and how long results typically take.

Step 03 — Course design

A plan with treatment types, session frequency, anticipated timeline, and pricing. Adjustable as your skin responds.

Common questions.

How many treatments will I need?

Most skin rejuvenation treatments work as a course — typically 3–6 sessions spaced weeks apart, with maintenance treatments thereafter.

Is there downtime?

Depends on the treatment. HydraFacial has none. Halo, MOXI, and Morpheus8 involve a few days of redness and peeling. We’ll outline exactly what to expect.

Is it suitable for my skin tone?

Many skin treatments work well across all skin tones, but some laser wavelengths carry higher risk for darker complexions. We’ll be honest about what’s safe and effective for you.

Will I see results after one session?

Some treatments (HydraFacial, light peels) show a noticeable difference after a single visit. Others (microneedling for scarring, deeper resurfacing) need multiple sessions.

What about at-home skincare?

We’ll always talk about at-home care. In-clinic treatments alone won’t deliver the results you want without a sensible home routine. We can recommend products — without selling them.
