A note about this site

About this demo.

Aurum Aesthetics is a fictional clinic website built to demonstrate Glowgau’s AI clinic assistant. Below is a transparent summary of what this site is, what it isn’t, and what we’ve deliberately chosen not to fabricate.

The short version.

This is a demo clinic website used to demonstrate Glowgau’s AI clinic assistant. It is not a real medical practice and does not provide medical advice.

What this site is

Aurum Aesthetics is a working example of how Glowgau’s AI clinic assistant would appear on the website of a real aesthetic clinic. It’s designed to:

  • Show what the widget looks like on a real clinic site
  • Demonstrate the kinds of questions the assistant can answer
  • Let prospective clients experience the consultation-request flow end to end
  • Help real clinics evaluate Glowgau with a realistic-feeling example

What this site is not

  • Not a real clinic. Aurum Aesthetics is fictional. We don’t have real premises, real staff, or real treatments available.
  • Not medical advice. Nothing on this site should be taken as medical advice. Real aesthetic decisions belong in a real consultation with a qualified clinician.
  • Not a transactional site. You can’t actually book a treatment through this demo. Forms and buttons are illustrative — they show what the experience would look like for a real clinic.

What we’ve deliberately chosen not to fabricate

We’ve worked to make the site feel real without crossing lines that would be misleading. Specifically:

  • No fake client reviews. Real medspas have reviews — we’ve left them off entirely rather than publish fictional testimonials.
  • No before-and-after photos claiming real results. Imagery on this site is stock photography from Unsplash. It’s not a record of real patient outcomes.
  • No fake medical credentials. Our team members are placeholder personas with realistic-but-fictional credentials. We don’t claim Allergan Diamond Provider status, board certifications we don’t have, or training from specific named instructors.
  • No fake tier claims. We don’t display “top-rated” or “as seen in Vogue” badges — even though real clinics often do.
  • No fake pricing. We deliberately don’t publish specific treatment prices, because prices vary by client and we don’t want to invent numbers.
  • No fake clinic names. The clinic name, addresses, phone numbers, and operational details are fictional. The 555-01XX telephone numbers shown are reserved by North American convention for fiction.

Treatment vocabulary we do use

You’ll see product and treatment names like Botox, Dysport, Xeomin, Juvederm, Restylane, Kybella, HydraFacial, Halo, MOXI, Morpheus8, BBL, CoolSculpting, Emsculpt NEO, and Emtone on this site. These are real, factual category names used across the medical-aesthetic industry — they describe what the treatments are, not endorsements or affiliations with the manufacturers. They appear on this demo site because they’re the vocabulary a real medspa would use.

Photography

All photography on this site is from Unsplash, a free stock-photo library. The portraits of our team members are stock models — they do not depict real medical professionals.

About Glowgau

Glowgau is a real company building an AI clinic assistant for aesthetic and wellness practices. This demo is one of the ways Glowgau shows prospective clinic clients what their own AI-powered website could look like. For more information about Glowgau, please contact the Glowgau team directly.
